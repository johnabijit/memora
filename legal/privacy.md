# Memora Privacy Notice
Effective: 24 October 2026 | Version: 2026-10-24-v1

Memora stores user-supplied memories, images, audio, documents, location details where granted, saved people/places, and account information to provide personal memory retrieval. Some records may contain personal information about other people; upload only where lawful and authorized.

## Processing and sharing
Memora uses authentication, database and file-storage infrastructure and may use AI services to answer questions. Data is processed to save, organize, search and answer user requests, maintain security and troubleshoot the service. External integrations must be explicitly authorized, with limited scopes and revocable access. Do not assume WhatsApp or third-party apps provide the same privacy guarantees as Memora.

## Security and access
Account access is authenticated; memory records should be restricted by per-user authorization. Network and service security risks cannot be eliminated. Report suspected unauthorized access promptly. Do not store passwords, access tokens or other secrets in memories.

## Retention and rights
Saved information remains until deleted by the user or according to a disclosed retention policy. Operational records and backups may have different retention periods; exact periods and a verified deletion/contact workflow must be published before commercial rollout. Users may request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

## Children
Memora is not designed for independent use by children without appropriate parental or guardian authorization. Before enabling child-focused features, age, guardian consent and applicable child-data requirements must be implemented.

## Contact and disclosures pending
Before a public commercial launch, publish the operator's verified contact details, applicable grievance contact, processors, processing locations, retention schedules and incident response procedures. This notice must be reviewed against applicable Indian data-protection law.
